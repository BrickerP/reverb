# Take Stack / 回响

[中文](README.md)

**Yesterday's you is today's consumable.**

You move. The weapons fire. The copy you leave behind wears out. The ending is a signature.

![Six paper clerks](docs/select.png)

Walk on the sheet first: [brickerp.github.io/reverb](https://brickerp.github.io/reverb/?lang=en). You from three seconds ago follows, then gets crumpled. Downloads are on [Releases](https://github.com/BrickerP/reverb/releases).

![Choices leave a signature](docs/story.png)

<details>
<summary>Six people</summary>

| Role | Skill | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Creased | Rewind position, Echo playback, and weapon cooldown | Start |
| Reject | No Echoes. A paper dash | Best stage 4 |
| Misprint | Swap places with the selected Echo | Clear once |
| Binder | Three bolts anchored on an Echo | Best stage 8 |
| Mender | Drop a repair station with limited uses | Best stage 3 |
| Overloader | Scrap the selected Echo. The burst scales with durability left | Best stage 7 |

</details>

<details>
<summary>Orders, endings, controls</summary>

The shop holds one work order at a time: salvage, audit, repair, rescue, recruit, credit. Buying an order means taking the job, not purchasing the danger.

After stage 12 you sign: release, leave together, or take over the bureau. Debt does not pick the ending.

Move with WASD. Role skill is Shift. Machines are E. Tap Space to scrap an Echo, hold it to pick another.

Numbers and saves are in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). Source needs Godot 4.7.2, then `make run`.
