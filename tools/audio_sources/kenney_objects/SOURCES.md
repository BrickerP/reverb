# Immutable CC0 object inputs

These nine WAVs are processed snapshots of Take Stack's Kenney CC0 palette at
commit `be0214e7dd3dd118caca5ed5e31bac6850cb5ef1`. They are inputs to the single
offline `tools/build_presentation_audio.py` producer; they are not game content
and the `tools/` export exclusion keeps them out of every PCK.

`sources.json` records byte hashes. Original packs, clip names and processing
provenance are documented in `audio/presentation/SOURCES.md`. License:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No new downloaded source or
actual paper recording is claimed by the derived granular material.
